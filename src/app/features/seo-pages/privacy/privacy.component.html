Introduction

QuickMaid aapki privacy ka respect karta hai. Ye policy batati hai ki hum kaunsa data kyon lete hain aur kaise protect karte hain — Information Technology (Reasonable security practices and procedures and sensitive personal data or information) Rules, 2011 aur Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ke framework ke saath align rehne ki disha mein (implementation details product maturity ke saath update ho sakti hain).

1. Data jo hum collect kar sakte hain

  • Identity & contact: naam, mobile, email, service address / landmark.
  • Booking metadata: slot, plan type, visit notes (non-sensitive), payment status (processor ke through).
  • Support: tickets, chat / email transcripts aap humein bhejte ho.
  • Technical: device/browser type, coarse timestamps, error logs (security + reliability).

2. Use cases

Service deliver karna, routing / dispatch, customer support, fraud prevention, aggregated analytics, product improvement, aur legal compliance (tax, lawful requests).

3. Sharing

Data trusted processors (SMS, payments, maps, cloud hosting) ke saath contracts ke under share ho sakta hai. Lawful government / court requests par disclosure ho sakti hai jahan applicable ho. Hum aapka personal data “sell” nahi karte mass marketing brokers ko.

4. Cookies & local storage

Session maintain, preferences, aur anti-abuse signals ke liye browser storage / cookies use ho sakte hain. Non-essential marketing cookies (agar introduce hon) se pehle consent UI add kiya jayega.

5. Retention

Records tax / legal obligations ke liye zaroori period tak retain ho sakti hain; operational logs rotate / minimise kiye jaate hain. Deletion requests jahan law allow karta hai, process Contact se initiate ho sakta hai.

6. Your rights

Access / correction / withdrawal of consent (where processing is consent-based) / deletion (subject to legal carve-outs) — requests verifiable identity ke saath handle kiye jayenge.

Security practices: access control, encryption in transit (HTTPS), vendor reviews — defence in depth approach; detailed infosec posture enterprise customers ke saath NDA ke under share ho sakti hai.

7. Grievance officer

Name, designation, aur contact window Contact page par publish kiye jayenge — grievance acknowledgement + resolution timelines applicable law ke mutabiq maintain karne ki koshish.

Data ke baare mein baat karni hai?

Grievance ya deletion request — verifiable identity ke saath humein likhein; official channels use karein.
