Introduction

Ye terms QuickMaid platform aur aapke beech service use ko clarify karte hain. Product evolve hote rahega — material changes par website / WhatsApp par reasonable notice dene ki koshish karenge; major changes par renewed acceptance maang sakte hain.

1. Acceptance

Booking request bhejne, website use karne, ya QuickMaid se communicate karne se aap in terms se agree maante ho. Agar agree nahi, kripya service use na karein.

2. Service nature

QuickMaid home cleaning aur related on-demand services facilitate karta hai. Exact deliverables plan, slot, add-ons, aur city norms ke hisaab se confirm hote hain — har visit ka scope booking confirmation / work order par clearly hona chahiye.

QuickMaid ek marketplace / facilitator hai — on-ground service delivery partners ke through hoti hai, jahan applicable law allow kare.

3. Bookings & cancellations

  • Slot confirmation availability aur partner capacity par depend karti hai.
  • Late cancellation / rescheduling / no-show fees agar applicable hon to booking flow ya rate card par disclose kiye jayenge.
  • Force majeure (weather, bandh, network outage) cases mein reschedule priority di ja sakti hai.

4. Payments

Payments authorized payment partners ke through process ho sakti hain. Taxes (GST) jahan lagoo hon, invoice / receipt rules applicable law ke mutabiq honge. Chargebacks / disputes payment provider + internal review ke through handle ho sakte hain.

5. Conduct & safety

Customers aur service partners dono se respectful conduct expected hai. Abuse, harassment, illegal activity, ya platform misuse par access terminate ho sakta hai — criminal matters ko authorities ke saath cooperate kiya ja sakta hai jahan law require kare.

6. Limitation of liability

Jitna local law allow kare, QuickMaid ki aggregate liability disputed booking / billing period ke fees paid tak capped ho sakti hai. Consequential / indirect damages exclude kiye ja sakte hain jahan legally permissible ho. Koi bhi clause aapko non-waivable statutory rights se override nahi karti.

7. Governing law

Disputes primarily Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India ke courts / consumer forums ke subject rahenge, subject to mandatory arbitration / jurisdiction rules jo law impose kare.

8. Contact

Legal / privacy questions ke liye Contact page — sirf official channels use karein.
