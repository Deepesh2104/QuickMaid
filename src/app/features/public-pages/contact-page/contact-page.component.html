Phishing se bachne ke 4 rules

Fraudsters kabhi-kabhi “cheap maid” ke naam par advance UPI maangte hain random apps par. QuickMaid sirf official flow follow karta hai — booking confirm hone ke baad hi pay, jahan policy allow ho.

  1. Sirf yahi page + inhi WhatsApp / phone / email ko bookmark karo.
  2. +91 98765 43210 waale alawa “new company number” par trust mat karo.
  3. Team kabhi OTP / UPI PIN nahi maangti — kabhi share mat karo.
  4. Doubt ho to yahi number par call karke verify karo, phir pay karo.
